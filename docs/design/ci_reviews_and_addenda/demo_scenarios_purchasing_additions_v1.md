## PD-3 — NEW beat: the Purchasing Proof Ledger (two-curve) + SILENCE (add to §2.3/§4, L-PUR)

Purchasing's version of the **DI-PROOF credibility linchpin**. Two adjacent curves on one screen:
- **Proof Curve** — gated incremental dollars, *allowed to fall*: *"$412 recovered — $310 you'd have missed, $102 you'd have caught."*
- **Competence Curve** — **time-to-competence**, rising even when dollars fall.

Caption: *"Every dollar we claim, we can defend — including the weeks we claim zero."* Spoken: *"We recovered less this quarter than last. In this product, that's the win — it means the leaks are gone. What's still climbing is how fast we get good on anything new."* **Class: NEAR** (gated-attribution surface). **New SILENCE beat:** after the honest $0 renders, stop — let them read a savings tool reporting *less* on purpose.

## PD-4 — NEW beat: "Not Yet" / The Quiet Week (the D-null + honesty moment)

The clean-operator answer, and the strongest trust moment in the cut. Two forms:
- **Not Yet** (gated I1 empty state): *"No supplier factor is reliably misleading yet — ~60 more verified deliveries before we'd stand behind that."*
- **The Quiet Week**: *"$0 incremental this week. Nothing leaked. Coverage 94%. We got faster at three things."*

**Class: LIVE** (no new math). This is the beat that proves the system can disprove its own story — it reinforces the whole positioning. Pairs with the §2.5 silence technique.
