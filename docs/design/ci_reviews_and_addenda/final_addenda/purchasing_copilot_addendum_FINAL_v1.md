## §8.2 — The two-curve Proof Ledger (upgrade the Weekly Recovered-Dollar Report) (v1 §B)
§8.2 today is a flat money trail carrying a **structural bug**: a working system removes the leakage it recovers, so recovered dollars *fall* in a healthy account. Split it:
- **B1. Proof Curve — hard dollars, allowed to fall.** Every claimed dollar passes an **incremental-attribution gate** first: counterfactual/holdout ("would a competent buyer, or your own baseline, have caught this?"), no double-counting, **realized-not-modeled.** Reads: *"$412 recovered — $310 you'd have missed (here's each), $102 you'd likely have caught."* Falling is success, and say so: *"We recovered less this quarter than last. That's what it looks like when it's working."*
- **B2. Competence Curve — the moat, allowed to accelerate.** Pin acceleration to **time-to-competence on situations never seen before** (new supplier/manager/menu/shock), **not** coverage %/accuracy/automation share (all saturate). *"First new supplier: 9 weeks to read a normal delivery. Latest: 11 days."* γ / re-convergence in kitchen language, weekly, non-saturating — the same metric that proves continuity.
- **B3. Attribution hierarchy** (more conservative as the claim strengthens): Flagged → Avoided → Realized → Incremental → Compounding. Insufficient evidence → **display $0**, not a modeled number.
- **B4. Honest $0 is a feature:** *"$0 incremental this week — nothing leaked; coverage 94%; we got faster at three things,"* while the competence curve keeps rising.
