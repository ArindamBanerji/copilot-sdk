# H-ENRICH dose-response design

## Pre-registered prediction

The primary prediction is that aggregate Day-1 floor lift, `floor_enriched − floor_unenriched`,
is monotonically increasing in relevance concentration κ, is approximately zero and sign-noisy at
κ=0, and becomes positive and sign-consistent at high κ. A monotone rise with a possible threshold
κ* confirms the targeted-enrichment claim. A flat, non-monotone, or positive-at-κ=0 curve falsifies
this concentration explanation. The rate check is secondary: k must remain within ±20% between
arms for the effect to be leveling rather than a rate change.

## Engineered GT construction

The fixed operating point is ε_firm=.35, C=6, A=4, d=6, μ0=`np.full((6,4,6), .5)`, seeds
42/123/777, and 1200 round-robin decisions. The concentration sweep is
`κ ∈ {0, .5, 1, 2, 4}`. Factor `f*=0` is fixed before execution. For each seed, a deterministic
seeded sign tensor `s[c,f]∈{-1,+1}` is generated. With action codes `[-1.5,-.5,.5,1.5]`, raw
profiles are:

`raw[c,a,f] = .5 + base * s[c,f] * action_code[a] * scale[f]`,

where `base=.02`, `scale[0]=1+κ`, and `scale[1:]=1`. The complete displacement is then globally
rescaled so `||GT−μ0||F=.35`. This produces exact uniform separations at κ=0 and a controlled
single-factor separation ratio at higher κ without clipping. `sep[f]` is measured after
construction as the mean absolute pairwise action separation and the chosen f* and ranking are
persisted. The GT is engineered independently of μ after μ0 is fixed; it is not generated by a
scorer.

## Arms and firewall

UNENRICHED uses σ=.08 on all factors. ENRICHED uses σ=.04 only on the shared f*=0 factor and
.08 elsewhere. Both arms receive identical GT, μ0, standard-normal stream, neutral exact-tie
stream, round-robin coverage, nearest-GT vector-only labeling rule, and update
`mu <- mu + .05*(f-mu)` on the oracle-labeled cell. Labels are not asserted equal. Enrichment
changes only observed vector noise.

## Metrics, persistence, and falsifiers

The aggregate floor is the average accuracy over the first 10 observations of each of all 24 cells.
Rate is the H-CURVE ten-decision block median-pairwise-slope k; its arm ratio must stay in [.8,1.2].
N is the first global decision whose rolling ten-decision accuracy reaches .70. Every seed×κ
persists GT, μ0, sep, f*, sigma vectors, shared random streams, decisions, accuracy, distance,
final μ, floor, k, N, and invariant assertions. The manifest is atomic and SHA-256 complete;
metrics are scorer-free recomputable.

The screen is n=3. Sign-consistent positive floor lift at the top κ recommends a 20-seed extension;
sign-inconsistent or negative lift is a null for this apparatus. F-FLOOR fires when the predicted
monotone/sign-consistent lift is absent; F-RATE fires when k changes beyond ±20% or is invalid.
