## F1/F2/F3 — fired? + deciding numbers

- **F1:** not fired as a pointwise contradiction because every γ_dist is censored.
  The binary prediction is nevertheless untestable in this run: 9/9 cells lack a primary
  distance-rate ratio. N_half γ alone is not promoted to the result.
- **F2:** not evaluable. γ_dist is missing in 9/9 cells, so θ-free versus θ-dependent
  direction agreement cannot be established.
- **F3:** **FIRED.** Every one of 18 phase trajectories has at least one raw increase;
  violation counts are shown above. The required centroid-distance monotonicity mechanism
  did not hold under the executed parametric protocol.

## Verdict — INVALIDATED

**H-CURVE: INVALIDATED on the pre-registered F3 condition** because centroid-distance
trajectories were not monotonically decreasing in any cell. The primary θ-free γ claim is
also INCONCLUSIVE due to universal half-distance censoring; N_half γ is not a substitute.
The result does not support the binary γ theorem on this run, and no EXP-G1 model-validity
claim was attempted.
